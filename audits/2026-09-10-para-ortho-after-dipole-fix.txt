9. CLOSING THE STEP CONVERGENCE
==================================================================

9a. The cause, read off CREM_SKIP_CENSUS.  Under the default
    stochasticElectricDipole model the orbit does NOT decay between
    photons -- the semi-major axis is flat to five digits for nine
    consecutive checkpoints, then one photon moves it by a factor of
    four (para index 2, master seed 42, s_max=0.30):

      #    t(ps)        a(m)        L(hbar)
      1     0.0000   1.0584e-10   1.00000
      5    63.4899   1.0583e-10   1.00000
      9   126.9774   1.0583e-10   1.00001
     10   142.8489   2.7439e-11   0.50944   <- photon
     15   144.2317   2.7435e-11   0.50955
     27   147.5482   2.7427e-11   0.50950

    Six sites nevertheless applied the sinking-envelope shape
    u(n)=u0(1-s)^(-2/3) within each skip: the checkpoint's elapsed
    proper time (1-s/2), the hazard's integralFactor, the identity's
    meanInSkipGrowth and its envelope reference, the photon's position
    in the skip and its quantum (energyRatio), and a sixth, duplicated
    copy of the elapsed-time formula in the lab-frame tail.

9b. Effect on the collapse time, one trajectory (para index 2, seed 42):

      s_max    before      after
      0.30     147.824     199.428
      0.20     163.715     198.970
      0.10     180.782     198.944
      0.045    191.427     199.747

    Before: monotonic, +29.5% and still climbing at the finest step.
    After:  flat to 0.4% over a 6.7x range of step size.

9c. The photon events themselves are now step-independent.  Times and
    semi-major axes at the two emissions, s_max=0.30 against 0.10:

      photon 1   t=186.7296 ps vs 186.7084 ps   a: 1.0583e-10 -> 3.528e-11
      photon 2   t=199.1727 ps vs 198.6764 ps   a: 3.527e-11  -> 7.91e-12

    0.011% and 0.25% apart, against 22% before.

9d. The convergence table recorded in the code at maximumJumpParameter
    (124.169 / 124.957 / 125.585 / 124.986 / 124.328 ps for s_max
    0.012..0.30, claimed flat to +/-0.6%) reproduces at no
    configuration this file has today.  It predates the present
    defaults and was carried forward unremeasured, which is how a real

10b. Where the default stop sits.  At threshold 150 (and at 50 and 20,

10c. THE TERMINAL L THE ESTIMATOR REPORTS CANNOT BE USED FOR THIS.
     Between emissions the spin-orbit transport changes L at fixed
     energy, and the result is not a bound Kepler orbit:

       state                          L (hbar)   h^2/(A a)
       right after photon 3            0.0983      1.0002
       reported at the default stop    0.2151      4.7898
       right after photon 4            0.0212      0.9936
       reported at the deep stop       0.4475    445.9

     h^2/(A a) is 1-e^2 for a bound orbit and cannot exceed 1.  The
     post-emission values satisfy that; the reported ones exceed it by
     factors of 4.8 and 446.  osculatingEccentricity clamps e^2 to
     zero, so nothing in the ordinary output shows it.  Recorded now
     as CremCollapseEstimate::terminalKeplerConsistency.

10d. Correction to what this file said in section 3.  The obstacle to
     answering "does L reach contact" was NOT that the retardation
     stop cuts the trajectory short -- it is one photon short, and
     lowering it answers the question.  The obstacle is that L is
     corrupted between emissions, so the quantity the estimator
     reports at the stop is not the pair's orbital angular momentum.
