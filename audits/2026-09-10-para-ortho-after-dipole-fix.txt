    Branch gap 147.834632 - 147.556519 = 0.278113 ps against a final
    checkpoint of 0.2768 ps: the two branches differ by exactly one.
    All six stop on the retardation limit and all fire 2 photons, so
    neither the stop cause nor the photon count is the discriminator.

8b. Every trajectory ends ON its final photon.  Binding before the last
    checkpoint, binding at the stop, and hbar*omega at the emission
    point (the census value carried down the skip by (1-s)^(-2/3) and
    omega ~ |E|^(3/2)):

    ortho idx2 (long)   26.23 eV -> 170.09 eV   jump 143.86 eV   hbar*omega 143.85 eV   ratio 1.0001
    ortho idx4 (short)  26.23 eV -> 167.92 eV   jump 141.69 eV   hbar*omega 141.68 eV   ratio 1.0001

    One photon carries five times the binding the pair had left, so it
    takes the orbit past the stop in a single step.  Emission is
    resolved only at checkpoint boundaries, so the reported collapse
    time is quantised in units of one final checkpoint.

    secular step.  Trace of para index 2, master seed 42, with

    0.17% para/ortho separation measured at s=0.30 is 150x smaller
