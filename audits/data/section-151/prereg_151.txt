stencil.  Reading further, the model already has what that needs and it is
not new algebra: RetardedSegmentPin exists precisely so that a GROUP of
retarded reads sees the same polynomial, and interpolatedChargeWithJerk
records why -- "a cubic is its own third-order Taylor series", so one
segment continued past its ends reproduces the interpolant exactly and
smoothly.  covariantDipoleGradientForce already pins its six spatial
probes for the identical reason, with the identical symptom measured
(a 33% jump in a central difference when one probe crossed a node).
So the repair is to pin the segment across the rate's three time samples.
The difference is then the derivative of one smooth cubic, which IS the
analytic derivative of the interpolant, and the limiter of audit 150
should become unnecessary.
