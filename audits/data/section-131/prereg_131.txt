THE QUANTITY.  For the Kepler problem the two actions are
  J_phi = 2 pi L,
  J_r   = 2 pi (K sqrt(mu/(2|E|)) - L),
so J_r + J_phi = 2 pi K sqrt(mu/(2|E|)) = n h with
  n = (K/hbar) sqrt(mu/(2|E|)),
which is the n_E the CREM_PHOTON_LADDER trace already prints.  Both are
built from the two osculating elements the estimator already carries
(specificEnergy, specificAngularMomentum), so no new dynamics is needed.

THE THREE QUESTIONS.
  Q1  Does one photon remove Delta J = h?
  Q2  Does the collapse stop where the total action is an integer
      multiple of h?
  Q3  Is --ground-state-floor the same object as an action floor at
      n = 1, and what does imposing it cost?

RULES, decided now.
R131a  Q1 is answered YES if the median |Delta n| over the photons of a
       cascade is inside 0.9..1.1.  The recorded ladder of 29b
       (2.000 -> 1.414 -> 0.910 -> 0.509 -> 0.228) says it will not be,
       so the measurement here has to ADD the reason and the law, not
       just the verdict: I will fit Delta n against the closed form the
       emission rule implies and report the agreement.
R131b  Q2 is answered YES if, over an ensemble of at least 12 stopped
       trajectories, more than 40% of the stopping actions fall within
       0.1 of an integer (a uniform fractional part gives 20%), OR if
       the stopping actions cluster with a spread below 0.1 h.
R131c  Q3 is answered YES if the floor's own clamp is algebraically the
       n = 1 action, i.e. if the energy it clamps to and L >= hbar are
       the two halves of J_r + J_phi = h.
R131d  COST OF THE FLOOR.  Record the action of the state production
       actually prepares and the action at the transit endpoint
       (0.005 a_pair).  If the prepared action is already h or below,
       an action floor at n = 1 forbids the collapse the model measures,
       and that has to be stated as the cost rather than buried.
R131e  The radial and azimuthal actions are reported SEPARATELY, because
       a photon could remove h from one without removing it from the
       total; audit 21b measured the angular part alone (0.42, 0.30,
       0.18, 0.16 hbar surrendered against 1 hbar demanded) and this
       section must reproduce those numbers from the new trace or
       explain the difference.

PREDICTION (mine, before the numbers).  Q1 no, with Delta n rising
toward 1 only for large n; Q2 no, continuous scatter; Q3 yes; and the
prepared action exactly h, so the floor deletes the collapse rather than
stopping it.
