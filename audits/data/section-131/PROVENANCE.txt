  run131_phi.txt      the azimuthal action per photon against audit 21b

  action_traces.tar.gz  the ACTION and PHOTON_LADDER lines of each run
                      (t131_err_<seed> default margin, long_err_<seed> the
                      1500 s budget, m300_/m50_ the shifted margins)
