WHAT 156e ASKED FOR.  Every ledger measurement since section 102 runs with
the radiation reaction disabled, so the far zone receives about four times
the energy the particles lose and the configuration creates 4.54 |U_dd|
per orbit from nothing -- four to eight times the residue being chased.
The fix is to test T + U + radiated with the reaction ENABLED, so that the
particles pay and the configuration's own floor drops below the residue.
The engine already computes both halves.

THE PROBE.  tools/velocity_dependent_energy gains a fifth ledger,
E1 + orbitalRadiatedEnergy, and a switch that enables the production
reaction model.  E1 is the ledger without the velocity-linear charge-dipole
term, which audit 145 established and 146 split out in code.

RULES, decided now.
R157a  THE CONFIGURATION IS USABLE if, with the reaction enabled, the range
       of E1 + radiated over one orbit falls below 0.2 |U_dd| -- i.e. below
       the 0.55 |U_dd| residue 155 left, by enough to attribute it.
R157b  THE COMPARISON IS REPORTED BOTH WAYS.  With the reaction disabled the
       same combination must be WORSE than E1 alone, because the radiated
       term is then added to a ledger that never paid it; if it is not, the
       reading of 156c is wrong and the section says so.
R157c  If the reaction changes the trajectory enough that the two
       configurations are not comparable, that is reported with the radial
       spans rather than glossed.
R157d  The verdict on the 0.55 |U_dd| residue is stated: attributable,
       still open, or smaller than the new floor.
R157e  No model file changes; the switch is in the probe.

PREDICTION (mine).  With the reaction on, E1 + radiated comes out near the
integration floor -- audit 145 measured 0.091 |U_dd| for the instantaneous
configuration -- so R157a passes and the residue becomes attributable for
the first time in this line.  With the reaction off, the same combination
is about 4.5 |U_dd| worse, which is 156c's number appearing from the other
side.
