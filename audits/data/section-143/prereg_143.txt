THE DEFECT.  Audit 91 withdrew both impositions on the mutual angle, so
menu items 1 and 2 (scenarios 2 and 3) now sample the IDENTICAL ensemble:
494/1506 on either side of the threshold, mean cosine -0.0038 and
|mu1+mu2|/mu = 1.3293 for both.  The menu entry became a label, the
paired comparison tool refuses to run, and the README still describes a
physics the default run does not produce.

THE REPAIR BEING MADE.  Menu 1 draws only states the model itself
classifies as p-Ps, menu 2 only those it classifies as o-Ps, and menu 5
keeps the free draw with identification after the fact.  This is
CONDITIONING on the model's own classification (dipoleAlignment >= 0.5),
not imposition of a value: no cosine is set, nothing is aligned by hand,
and the accepted states are exactly the states the free draw already
produces.  It is therefore not the quantization audit 91 removed, and the
section must not be read as restoring it.

RULES, decided now.
R143a  After the change, menu 1 must classify as ParaPositronium in 100%
       of prepared states and menu 2 as OrthoPositronium in 100%; any
       leakage is a wiring error, not a statistic.
R143b  The two channels must now differ in the quantities that were
       identical: report the mean cosine and <|mu1+mu2|/mu> per channel
       over at least 2000 prepared states each, against the 1.3293 and
       -0.0038 both gave before.
R143c  Menu 5 must be UNCHANGED, bit for bit, since the free draw is what
       it keeps: the same seed must give the same prepared state as before
       the change.
R143d  The acceptance rates must match the measured shares -- about 24.7%
       for para and 75.3% for ortho -- or the conditioning is not drawing
       from the distribution it claims to condition.
R143e  The cost is reported: extra RNG draws per prepared state, and the
       fact that every seeded number quoted for menus 1 and 2 moves.
R143f  The audit's standing statement that the hyperfine, contact and M1
       sections were never re-measured after 91 is NOT repaired by this
       change and must be repeated, not quietly dropped: conditioning
       restores two ensembles, it does not restore cos = -1, so the exact
       cancellation |mu1+mu2| = 0 that the M1 result stood on is still
       reached with probability zero.

PREDICTION (mine).  R143a and R143d hold by construction; the conditioned
channels give mean cosines near +0.75 and -0.25 and <|m|/mu> near 1.87
and 1.15; R143c holds because menu 5's code path is untouched.
