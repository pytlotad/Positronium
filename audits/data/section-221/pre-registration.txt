PRE-REGISTERED, audit 221, before any orbit was integrated.

199c/199d established that a circular orbit radiates only the
fundamental, its k=2 residual being 3.3802e-05 with the moments
on and 5.2869e-05 with them off, the latter equal to beta^2 =
5.3253e-05 at a_pair to a ratio of 0.993 -- i.e. the 'harmonic'
of a circular orbit is the relativistic correction and not
Kepler geometry.  200c then validated the Bessel closed form
against the model to 2-8 parts in 1e4.  So the edge is sharp
and computable.  TWO definitions, both registered here.

EDGE A -- where the Kepler harmonic overtakes the relativistic
floor, i.e. where the k=2 content stops being beta^2 and starts
being eccentricity.  Small-e expansion of the Bessel form gives
A2/A1 -> 2e, so the crossing is at
    e_A = beta_rel^2 / 2,  beta_rel^2 = K/(mu c^2 r),
hence e_A proportional to 1/r: the edge moves UP as the orbit
tightens.  Predicted, with the moments off:
    r =  1.000000 a_pair   beta_rel^2 = 5.3251e-05   e_A = 2.6626e-05
    r =  0.100000 a_pair   beta_rel^2 = 5.3251e-04   e_A = 2.6626e-04
    r =  0.010000 a_pair   beta_rel^2 = 5.3251e-03   e_A = 2.6626e-03
    r =  0.001826 a_pair   beta_rel^2 = 2.9156e-02   e_A = 1.4578e-02
With the moments ON the floor is 3.3802e-05 instead, a factor
0.635 lower, so e_A should drop by the same factor.

EDGE B -- where the second harmonic overtakes the fundamental
in radiated power.  Parseval holds (200d), so the amplitude
ratio squared IS the power ratio and the crossing is A2/A1 = 1.
Solved from the closed form:
    e_B = 0.540055
and for the third and fourth harmonics against the fundamental:
    k = 3 overtakes k = 1 at e = 0.599048
    k = 4 overtakes k = 1 at e = 0.638715
    k = 5 overtakes k = 1 at e = 0.668015

PREDICTION.  The model reproduces e_B to the 2-8e-04 of 200c,
and reproduces e_A's 1/r scaling.  Edge A is the physically
interesting one: below it a Delta n = 2 transition has no
classical analogue in this model at all, because what sits at
k = 2 is a relativistic correction to a Delta n = 1 orbit.

CORRECTION made before any orbit was integrated: the first version
of this file used beta^2 = K/(2 mu c^2 r), which is the individual
particle's, and came out a factor two below 199d's 5.3253e-05.  The
quantity 199d measured is the RELATIVE beta, K/(mu c^2 r).  The
probe measures the circular floor at each radius directly rather
than taking either from this file, so the closed form is a
prediction to be checked and not an input.
