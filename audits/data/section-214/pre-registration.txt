THREE OUTCOMES, named in advance.
  A. The cos delta shape survives with a shifted amplitude: the 24 rows
     at s=0.075, sorted by delta, are again monotone from 90 to 145 deg
     with at most one or two exceptions, and seeds 1, 8, 17, 20 are no
     longer separable as a cluster.  Then the shape is physical and 213h
     stands, with its amplitude remeasured.
  B. The shape is gone: the s=0.075 ratios show no monotone trend in
     delta (Spearman |rho| over 90-180 deg below about 0.5).  Then 213h
     was an artefact of the coarse step and must be withdrawn, and the
     only surviving claim is the sign and order of magnitude of the mean.
  C. The mean itself moves outside 213's error bar, i.e. the s=0.075
     mean lands outside 9.2994e-04 +- 2 x 2.9847e-04 = [3.3e-04,
     1.53e-03].  213f's four-seed mean moved from 1.4514e-03 to
     9.7523e-04 on the same refinement, so a move is expected; C is
     about whether 213's quoted interval was honest, and it fails if the
     new mean lands outside it.
Outcomes A/B and C are independent: either can happen with either.

I EXPECT B and not-C: the four points above already break the curve's
amplitude, and the ensemble mean was much more stable under refinement
than any individual seed (213f: sd fell from 2.9332e-03 to 1.1581e-03
while the mean moved by a third of that).
