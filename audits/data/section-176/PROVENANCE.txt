No pre-registration: every column is either exactly zero or it is not, and no
threshold enters the conclusion.  The external-field row is a positive
control chosen in advance of reading the drift rows, so that a table of zeros
could not be mistaken for a probe that cannot see anything.

The probe is built against the production headers.  Every case prepares
ortho exactly -- secondProperDipole = -secondMagneticMoment along the same
direction as the first -- and varies only the tilt, the eccentricity, the
centre-of-mass velocity added to both particles, or gExternalMagneticField.
The three columns are the worst value over one orbit of
|w1-w2|/|w1| with w from thomasBmtDipoleDerivatives, |mu1+mu2| normalized by
mu1 + mu2, and |d(mu1+mu2)/dt| normalized by the sum of the two moments' own
rates.  Reaction is disabled, tolerance 1e-09, 500 samples per orbit.

velocity at zero is read from crem_trajectory.hpp's own comment and was not
remeasured.
