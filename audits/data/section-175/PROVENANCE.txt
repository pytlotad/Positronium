No pre-registration: the section is a size check on a claim, read directly
off a scan, with no threshold involved.

Both probes are built against the production headers.  They evaluate
pairDipoleInteractionEnergy twice on the same separation -- once with
state.firstDipole/secondDipole, the LAB moments synchronizeCovariantDipoles
produces, and once with firstProperDipole/secondProperDipole -- and take the
range of each over one orbit, together with the range of
conservedParticleEnergy.  Everything is normalized by k/r0 =
pairCoulombStrength / r0 with r0 the preparation radius, so the columns are
directly comparable with the claim's units.

Reaction is disabled and the forces are instantaneous, which is the
configuration the tree's own comment at conservedParticleEnergy identifies as
the one where an instantaneous ledger could be exact.  The retarded
comparison was started and did not finish; 175f says so and no number from it
appears.
