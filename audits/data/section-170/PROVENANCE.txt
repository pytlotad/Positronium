No pre-registration.  The survival window 0.5 <= final/initial <= 2 was
chosen after seeing the 25.5 of the disrupted run and is stated as a choice
in 170h; every raw row is kept here so a different window can be applied
without rerunning anything.

ROW format: ROW <seed> <scale> <r/a_pair> <P_abs> <P_rad> <orbits completed>
<final/initial separation>.  The last field is absent in the radius ensemble,
which was taken before it existed.

tools/zpf_power_balance.cpp gained the orbits argument, the single-radius
mode, the ROW output and the survival column in this section.  The exponent
fit is an ordinary least squares of log(power) on log(radius) over the four
radii, computed from the same rows.
