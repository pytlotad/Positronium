WHAT IS LEFT.  Section 145 decomposed the ledger's drift into 3.98 |U_dd|
per orbit of spurious velocity-linear term and 1.13 |U_dd| of
retarded-versus-instantaneous mismatch.  The first is explained and split
out in code (146).  The second has a named candidate and an instrument:
it should be the energy stored in the bound field between the particles,
the analogue of the field MOMENTUM audit 121 integrated.

THE QUANTITY.  The cross terms of the field energy,
  U_field = integral ( eps0 E1.E2 + B1.B2/mu0 ) dV,
whose self parts diverge at each particle and renormalize its mass, and
whose static limit is exactly the Coulomb interaction energy the ledger
already carries.  What the ledger cannot carry is its RETARDED value, so
the comparison is between the range of U_field over an orbit and the
range of the instantaneous interaction terms over the same orbit; their
difference is what 145e attributed to retardation.

RULES, decided now.
R155a  THE LEDGER IS CLOSED if the retarded U_field's range over one orbit
       differs from the instantaneous interaction energy's range by
       1.13 |U_dd| within a factor 2, i.e. between 0.57 and 2.26.
R155b  A SHORTFALL IS REPORTED AS A NUMBER.  If the difference is smaller,
       the remaining piece is named with its size and the ledger stays open
       by that amount; the section does not claim closure on a partial
       match.
R155c  CONVERGENCE MUST BE SHOWN, not assumed: the cumulative integral
       against the cut-off radius, and the same integral at two grid
       resolutions agreeing to 10%.  Audit 121's own history horizon
       applies -- beyond about 15 r* the retarded time leaves the stored
       history -- so the outer radius is bounded and that bound is stated.
R155d  The self terms are not computed and not estimated; only the cross
       terms are, which is the same choice audit 121 made and for the same
       reason.
R155e  No model file changes.

PREDICTION (mine).  The cross energy's static part will track the Coulomb
term closely, so the difference will be dominated by the retarded
correction, of order (v/c)^2 times the interaction energy.  At 1 r* with
beta about 0.17 that is a few percent of |U_C|, and |U_dd| is 1.4e-06 of
|U_C| there, so the retarded correction is thousands of |U_dd| -- far more
than 1.13.  I therefore expect R155a to FAIL in the direction of excess,
and the interesting number to be how much of that excess cancels between
the orbit's phases.
