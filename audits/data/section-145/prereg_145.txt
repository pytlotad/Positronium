THE DEFECT, RECORDED TWICE AND NEVER MEASURED.  The model's ledger is
  E = T + U_Coulomb + U_dd + U_chargeDipole + U_Darwin + U_constraint,
and three of those terms depend on VELOCITY, each differently:
  (a) U_chargeDipole is -p.E with p = (v x mu)/c^2, LINEAR in v;
  (b) U_Darwin goes as v1.v2, QUADRATIC in v;
  (c) U_dd is evaluated on the LAB moments, which synchronizeCovariantDipoles
      boosts, so it carries v through the boost.
For a Lagrangian L = T - U(r,v) the conserved quantity is
H = sum v.(dL/dv) - L, not T + U, and the two differ exactly in those
terms: a velocity-LINEAR potential drops out of H entirely, and a
velocity-quadratic one enters with the opposite sign.  So the ledger may be
adding to the conserved energy quantities that do not belong to it.

THE TEST.  Along one engine orbit, compute four ledgers on the SAME states:
  E0 = the model's ledger as it stands;
  E1 = E0 without the charge-dipole term (the velocity-linear one);
  E2 = E1 with the Darwin term's sign flipped (2 U_Darwin subtracted);
  E3 = E2 with U_dd evaluated on the PROPER moments instead of the lab ones.
Report each one's range and its end-minus-start drift, in units of |U_dd|.

RULES, decided now.
R145a  A variant EXPLAINS the drift if its range falls below 10% of E0's on
       the same orbit.  The first variant that does is the answer, and the
       later ones are then refinements rather than the explanation.
R145b  If no variant falls below 50% of E0's range, the velocity-dependent
       terms are NOT the source and the section says so; the candidate is
       then the retarded-versus-instantaneous mismatch named in audit 125e.
R145c  The comparison must be on the SAME trajectory, not on re-integrated
       ones: the variants are read off the same states so that no difference
       can come from the dynamics diverging.
R145d  Report what this does to the ~1 |U_dd| per orbit residual that the
       audit has carried open since section 104.
R145e  No model file changes in this section.  If a variant wins, changing
       the ledger is a separate decision with its own measurement.

PREDICTION (mine).  E1 wins: the charge-dipole term is linear in v, does no
work, and cannot appear in H, and audit 105 measured it as the largest
single contribution to the force sector too (88%).  I expect E1's range
below 10% of E0's, the Darwin flip to matter at the percent level because
it is O(v^2/c^2), and the proper-moment variant to matter least.
