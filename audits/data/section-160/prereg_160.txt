PRE-REGISTRATION, audit section 160, PARTIAL.  Written 2026-09-24, before
the cut-off test of R160c below was run and AFTER the grid-refinement
measurements of 160b were already in hand.  That asymmetry is stated here
rather than hidden: this file does not pre-register the whole section.

WHY ONLY PART OF IT IS PRE-REGISTERED.  The grid-refinement test has no
free parameter to choose after the fact.  A quadrature error either falls
when the node count rises or it does not, and the conclusion is read off
the sequence without comparing it to a threshold anyone picked.  There was
nothing for a pre-registration to protect, so none was written, and the
numbers were computed first.  The cut-off test below is different: it
needs a tolerance, so the tolerance is fixed now.

WHAT 159 CLAIMED.  Audit 159e attributed E6's converged 0.0094 |U_dd| to
"the field integral's own quadrature noise", reasoning from 155c's 0.34%
static residue scaled to the interaction energy at 3 r*.  If that is right
the correction cannot be tested; if it is wrong the correction is
measurable and 157's question is open again.

WHAT 160b ALREADY MEASURED (numbers in grids.txt and orbit_grids.txt).
The static residue holds at 0.0206 |U_dd| across a 27-fold rise in node
count, and E6 at 3 r* moves by 4.7e-05 when the grid is refined eightfold.

WHAT REMAINS, and the rule for it, decided now.
R160c  THE OUTER CUT-OFF IS NOT THE FLOOR if E6 at 3 r*, 48 samples, grid
       48x12x24, changes by less than 2.0e-04 |U_dd| between outer radii of
       8, 12 and 15 r*.  2.0e-04 is a quarter of the 0.000848 |U_dd| residue
       audit 159f left unexplained: a floor above that would matter to the
       energy line, and one below it would not.
       IF IT FAILS, the cut-off and not the grid is the instrument's limit,
       159e's conclusion stands for a reason 159e did not give, and this
       section reports that instead.
R160d  159e IS CORRECTED only if BOTH the grid test and R160c pass, so that
       the quadrature error is bounded below the residue it was said to
       swamp.  A single passing test is not enough.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED EITHER WAY.  Nothing here measures whether the
retarded correction is the right physical term.  The question is only
whether the instrument can see it.  The 15 r* row is at the edge of the
stored history (155b, 121), so a failure there may be the horizon rather
than the cut-off, and would be reported as such.
