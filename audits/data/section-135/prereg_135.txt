PRE-REGISTRATION, audit section 135 (does the model's M1 channel see the
libration, and does it damp it).  Written before any number of this
section was computed.
written 2026-09-18T19:11:02Z

THE QUESTION.  The mutual angle librates (68 GHz by the consecutive-extrema
definition of 115, 90.6 GHz by the whole-cycle one of 129) and the pair's
total moment swings with it, |S1+S2| running 0.37-0.66 hbar.  That is an
oscillating magnetic moment, i.e. an M1 source.  Does the model's own M1
channel carry it, and does the radiation damp the libration?  If it does
not, the libration amplitude that 113 and 115 rest on has no sink.

WHAT THE CODE ALREADY HAS, read before measuring:
  coherentMagneticDipoleOrbitAveragedEmission gives
  P_M1 = mu0 <|d2(mu1+mu2)/dt2|^2> / (6 pi c^3), built from the two BMT
  rates at the orbital phase nodes, and CREM_M1_SHARE prints it beside E1
  per checkpoint.  It is gated on isStochastic, so under the production
  reaction model (individual Landau-Lifshitz, the default since audit 109)
  it is not evaluated at all.

RULES, decided now.
R135a  THE CHANNEL IS CLOSED if the M1 power the model books agrees, within
       a factor 2, with the power recomputed from the TRACE's own total
       moment, P = mu0 |d2(mu1+mu2)/dt2|^2/(6 pi c^3), differentiated
       numerically along the same trajectory at the same checkpoints.
R135b  THE LIBRATION IS DAMPED if the M1 energy radiated over one libration
       period exceeds 1% of the libration's own energy, E_lib = J omega_lib
       with J = 0.0985 hbar from audit 129.  Below that the amplitude is
       not inflated by a missing sink, because the sink cannot act.
R135c  PRODUCTION.  If the production configuration books ZERO M1 power,
       that is stated plainly: the channel would then be not merely small
       but absent from the trajectories 113 and 115 were measured on.
R135d  Report the damping time E_lib/P_M1 against the 30.672 ps collapse
       and against the libration period itself.
R135e  The numerical second derivative must be shown to be resolved: the
       substep spacing against the libration period, and the same quantity
       at two stencil widths, agreeing to 10%.  Otherwise the comparison of
       R135a is reported as an order-of-magnitude statement.

PREDICTION (mine).  The two powers agree in order, since the model's
second derivative is built from the same two rates whose beat IS the
libration; the damping is negligible by many orders, so R135b fails and
the amplitude is not inflated; and production books exactly zero because
of the isStochastic gate.
