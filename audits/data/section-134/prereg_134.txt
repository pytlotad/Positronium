THE CLAIM BEING SHARPENED.  Section 116b measured that keeping ONLY the
partner's charge field in the precession conserves |S1+S2| exactly, and
116c built an argument on it.  That was one pair: equal masses, opposite
charges.

WHY I EXPECT THE SYMMETRY TO SURVIVE FOR mu+mu-, AGAINST THE PREMISE OF
THE REQUEST.  In the centre-of-mass frame the field each particle sees
from the partner's moving charge is
  B_1 ~ q2 (v2 x r_hat21)/r^2,   B_2 ~ q1 (v1 x r_hat12)/r^2,
and with EQUAL MASSES v1 = -v2 and r_hat12 = -r_hat21, so with opposite
charges B_2 = -B_1.  The precession rate carries q_i/m_i, so
  omega_1 ~ (q1/m) B_1,  omega_2 ~ (q2/m) B_2 = (-q1/m)(-B_1) = omega_1.
The equality therefore rests on equal masses AND opposite charges, not on
the species.  mu+mu- has both, so it should conserve |S| exactly as e+e-
does; only p+e-, where the mass ratio is 1836, breaks it.  If mu+mu-
drifts, this reading of the mechanism is wrong and the section says so.
