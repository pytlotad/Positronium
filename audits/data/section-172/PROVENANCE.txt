No pre-registration.  The prediction 6(1-p) was written down before the scan
was run, from the band amplitude's omega^2 law, and is reported as failing;
no threshold was involved in reading that.

Row format here is ROW <p> followed by the tool's own fields:
ROW <p> <seed> <scale> <r/a_pair> <P_abs> <P_rad> <orbits> <final/initial>
<band frequency>.  The leading <p> is prepended by the driver so the file is
self-describing.

The bootstrap resamples the 16 seeds with replacement 2000 times, refits both
exponents on each resample and takes the 16th and 84th percentiles of their
difference.  It is run on this file and on audit 170's grid_rows.txt with the
same code, which is what makes 172e's comparison between them meaningful.

Source change: modules/electrodynamics.hpp, zeroPointDriveFrequency
generalized to the one-parameter family.  CREM_ZPF_RIDE_EXPONENT and
CREM_ZPF_REFERENCE_FREQUENCY are new; CREM_ZPF_FIXED_FREQUENCY keeps its
audit 171 meaning as the p = 0 shorthand.  All three unset leaves the
production band bit for bit and the validation suite unmoved.
