ORDER OF WORK, STATED PLAINLY.  Four measurements were made BEFORE this
was written, in an exploratory sequence, and are reported as such:
  M1 stencil convergence: 3.2866e-06 at 9 nodes/span 2 against 3.2894e-06
     at 512/40, i.e. 0.09% over a 57x refinement -- not numerical;
  M2 sector: with both moments zeroed, |F1+F2| = 0.000000e+00 exactly at
     beta 0.01, 0.1 and 0.2 -- the charge-charge retarded interaction is
     reciprocal to the bit, and the whole residual is dipole-sector;
  M3 power in the moment: scaling both moments by 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 gives
     8.225e-07, 1.645e-06, 3.290e-06, 6.580e-06, 1.316e-05 -- exactly
     linear, so it is the CHARGE-MOMENT interaction, not moment-moment;
  M4 with CREM_NO_HIDDEN_MOMENTUM_FORCE the residual returns to FIRST
     order in beta (9.178e-05, 9.254e-04, 1.901e-03; slopes 1.004 and
     1.038) against third order with it.

THE NAMING THIS SUGGESTS.  The residual is the third-order term of the
charge-moment momentum balance whose first-order term is the hidden
momentum (mu x E)/c^2 that audit 124 installed.  The model carries that
series to first order only; the second order is absent because the series
is odd in beta, and what is left is the third.

RULES, decided now.
R142a  THE NAMING IS ACCEPTED only if an OUT-OF-SAMPLE prediction holds:
       the residual at beta = 0.05 and beta = 0.4, at the same geometry
       and stencil, must follow |F1+F2|/|F1| = c beta^3 with c between
       0.28 and 0.34, the band the three measured speeds span.  Predicted
       before running: 4.0e-07 N at beta = 0.05 and 2.4e-04 N at 0.4.
R142b  If either point falls outside that band, the naming is reported as
       CONSISTENT BUT NOT ESTABLISHED, and the section says which point
       broke it.
R142c  The size at the production orbit is reported, so the naming carries
       a consequence: beta at a_pair is 0.0036487, so the prediction there
       is c beta^3 of |F1|.
R142d  No model file is changed.  The moment-scale switch added to the
       probe is a tool-side diagnostic.
