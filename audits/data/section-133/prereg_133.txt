WHAT IS BEING CLOSED.  Sections 131 and 132 used the Kepler closed form
J_r = 2 pi (K sqrt(mu/2|E|) - L), which went NEGATIVE along the collapse
(-0.0054 typically, -0.0158 at the margin-50 deep end) -- impossible for a
real radial action, so the label was wrong there.  132 measured the source
(the dipole term, zero under ablation) but assumed the orbit is circular in
the model's own potential, which is the same statement as the gap being
small.  This section drops that assumption and integrates

  J_r = 2 integral_{r1}^{r2} p_r dr,   p_r = mu sqrt(2 h(r)),
  h(r) = eps + K/r - u_dd(r) - u_so(r,L) - L^2/(2 r^2),

the same h the estimator's dipoleAwarePeriapsis solves, between the model's
own turning points.  The trace is extended to print both moments and the
orbit normal at every checkpoint, so the potential is the one the
trajectory actually had rather than a fixed geometry.

RULES, decided now.
R133a  THE INTEGRATOR IS VALIDATED if, with the dipole terms zeroed, the
       numerical J_r matches the Kepler closed form to 1e-8 relative at
       eccentricities 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9.  Below that accuracy nothing else
       in this section is reported.
R133b  THE CIRCULARITY ASSUMPTION of 131-132 HOLDS if the measured
       J_r/h stays below 0.02 at every checkpoint of the collapse, which is
       the size of the Kepler gap it was compared with.
R133c  INCONSISTENT STATES.  If a checkpoint's (eps, L) has no accessible
       radial region in the model's potential, that is an inconsistency
       between the osculating elements and the potential.  Their count and
       radii are reported, not smoothed over.
R133d  WHICH NUMBERS MOVE.  Report the total (J_r + J_phi)/h at the prepared
       state and at the stop.  If they differ from 131's 1.000000000 and
       0.0962 by more than 1%, that is a correction to 131 and must be
       stated as one.
R133e  THE SIGN LEFT OPEN IN 132e.  J_r cannot be negative, so the question
       is its size: if J_r/h comes out of order 0.005-0.016 at the deep-end
       states, the true radial action accounts for the Kepler gap; if it
       comes out near zero, the gap is purely a label artefact and the orbit
       is genuinely circular in the model's potential.  Both answers are
       reportable; what is not allowed is to call it either without the
       integral.

PREDICTION (mine, before the numbers).  The integrator validates; J_r/h
stays below 1e-3 in the outer region and rises at the deep end but stays
under 0.02, so R133b holds; a few deep-end states may come out
inconsistent; and 131's two headline actions move by well under 1%, since
at a_pair the dipole is 3.3e-06 of Coulomb.
