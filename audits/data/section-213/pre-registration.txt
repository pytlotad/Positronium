Model.  The preparation is a circular orbit at a_pair with normal zhat,
identical for every seed; the seed sets only the direction of mu1.  The
dipole-dipole energy is  [mu1.mu2 - 3(mu1.rhat)(mu2.rhat)] / r^3, which
for quantized moments mu2 = +-mu1 is  +-mu^2 [1 - 3 cos^2 theta] / r^3.
With mu1 at polar angle delta from the normal and rhat sweeping the
equator, cos theta = sin(delta) cos(phi - phi0), so

    < 1 - 3 cos^2 theta >  =  1 - (3/2) sin^2 delta.

It vanishes at the magic angle sin^2 delta = 2/3, delta = 54.7356 deg.
