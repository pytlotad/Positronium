THE SETTING.  Audit 143 gave the two channels back by conditioning, and
audit 140 showed that CREM_SPIN_MAGNITUDE together with CREM_AXIAL_SPIN
makes the orbit surrender exactly one hbar per photon, ending the cascade
after one emission at L = 0.  Both are switched on here, for the
measurement only: 140 concluded they must not be defaults and this
section does not overturn that.

THE QUESTION.  Is there a knife edge -- a configuration in which the
channel LABEL and the emitted MULTIPLICITY are the same division?

RULES, decided now.
R144a  A KNIFE EDGE EXISTS if P(2 gamma | menu 1) > 0.9 and
       P(2 gamma | menu 2) < 0.1 in some configuration measured here.
R144b  A PARTIAL EDGE is recorded if the label agrees with the drawn
       multiplicity in more than 90% of trajectories overall.  Below that
       the separation is reported as a number and not as an edge.
R144c  Report the PREPARED and the TERMINAL measure separately, since
       audit 113 measured that the dynamics tilts the terminal one, and
       the spin switches change the trajectory that does the tilting.
R144d  If the closed-angular-momentum runs differ from the default ones
       only through the terminal measure, say so: then the switches act on
       the knife edge through the trajectory and not through the draw.
R144e  Nothing here becomes a default.  The section reports what the two
       switches do to this observable, with audit 140's verdict intact.

PREDICTION (mine).  No knife edge.  The conditioned ensembles have mean
cosines +0.7508 and -0.2461, so E[w] should come out near 0.875 and 0.377
at preparation, giving label agreement near 0.66 overall -- well short of
R144b -- and the terminal measure should sit closer to the prepared one
with the switches on than without, because the cascade ends after one
photon and the angle has less time to drift.
