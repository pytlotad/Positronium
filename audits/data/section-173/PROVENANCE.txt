The probe evaluates r = n^2 hbar^2/(mu K) from the model's own
pairReducedMass and pairCoulombStrength and compares it with
pairBohrRadius(activePair) * n^2, then computes mu v r / hbar on the n = 1
circle.  Both quantities come from the shipped headers; nothing is entered
by hand.

The collapse times quoted in 173c are README's stamped figures (--level 1
and --level 2 with the ground-state floor, seed 42, runs 3, budget 1200 s)
and were not remeasured here.  The claim that "de Broglie" appeared nowhere
in the tree before this section is from
  grep -rniE "de ?broglie|debroglie" --include=*.hpp --include=*.cpp
       --include=*.md --include=*.ap --include=*.txt .
which returned nothing.
