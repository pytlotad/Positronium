THE DEFECT, from section 149.  hiddenMomentumRateForce takes dE/dt by a
one-sided three-point backward difference over
derivativeStep = max(1e-4 r, 1e-3 cutoff)/c, which at 1 r* is 6.36e-26 s
-- about 1/1500 of the history's own node spacing.  Where the stencil
straddles a node crossing, the retarded field's C0 discontinuity (+4.01%
in the dipole field's electric part, measured) is divided by that tiny
step and becomes 15.40 times the Coulomb force.  Every nonzero tilt at
r <= 2.5 r* then fails the step-accuracy test at step 6 to 9 of 256.

THE REPAIR.  The stencil keeps its second-order form where the field is
smooth and refuses to amplify a jump where it is not.  With
  d1 = (E(0) - E(-h))/h,   d2 = (E(-h) - E(-2h))/h,
the current estimate is exactly (3 d1 - d2)/2.  Componentwise, that is
kept when the two halves agree -- |d1 - d2| <= theta max(|d1|,|d2|) --
and replaced by the smaller-magnitude half (minmod, zero on a sign
disagreement) when they do not.  Smooth fields have d1 - d2 = O(h), so
the test passes and nothing changes; a straddle drives the ratio to 1 and
the limiter engages.  theta = 0.5 is fixed here, before measuring.
CREM_HIDDEN_RATE_UNLIMITED restores the present stencil.
