PRE-REGISTRATION, audit section 140 (the per-photon angular momentum:
enabling CREM_SPIN_MAGNITUDE, or measuring why not).  Written before any
number of this section was computed.
written 2026-09-18T20:27:03Z

THE DEFECT.  Energy leaves in quanta hbar omega while angular momentum is
removed by the classical secular law, so each photon demands 1 hbar and
the orbit surrenders 0.42, 0.30, 0.18, 0.16 (audit 21b).  Sharper: before
the first emission L = 1.0000 hbar, so a photon carrying its own hbar
would land the orbit at L = 0, where E1 is forbidden; the model takes
0.4227 hbar and emits three more.  The whole cascade past the first photon
exists because L is not conserved photon by photon.

WHAT THE CODE ALREADY SAYS, read before measuring.  CREM_SPIN_MAGNITUDE
takes the subtraction from the photon's own spin and closes J: the
surrendered fraction goes from 0.266 (range 0.158-0.423) to 0.879 (range
0.451-2.305).  The old objection -- unphysical elements -- is already
marked stale in the comment, with a remeasurement showing e^2 never
negative over ten emissions on four seeds.  The CURRENT reason it is not
the default is cost: the orbit becomes eccentric (e = 0.844 after the
first photon on seed 42), the eccentricity factor is then 30.6, and
trajectories censor on the default wall-clock budget.  The comment asks
for an ensemble rather than four seeds, and that is what this section is.

RULES, decided now.
R140a  The switch BECOMES THE DEFAULT if, over at least 8 seeds:
       (a) the mean surrendered fraction of the photon's hbar is above
           0.8, against 0.266 today;
       (b) no emission produces e^2 < 0; and
       (c) at least half the seeds complete their cascade within a budget
           of 1800 s, ten times what the cascade tools use today.
R140b  Whatever moves in the cascade numbers -- collapse time, photon
       count, stopping cause -- is reported as a correction, not buried.
       The audit's cascade-dependent numbers are expected to move.
R140c  PRODUCTION MUST BE BIT-IDENTICAL.  Since audit 109 production runs
       individual Landau-Lifshitz with photons off, so this switch cannot
       touch it; the collapse transit of seed 42 must reproduce to all
       printed digits or the change is reverted rather than explained.
R140d  If the cost makes (c) fail, the switch is NOT defaulted, and the
       section reports the measured cost as the reason -- with the
       ensemble the comment asked for, so the question is closed either
       way instead of being left to the next reader.
R140e  This change does NOT address the energy-sector remark that the
       dipole potential is velocity-dependent and KE+U is therefore not
       what the dynamics conserves.  That is a separate defect and the
       section says so rather than letting the fix be over-claimed.

PREDICTION (mine).  (a) and (b) pass, since the comment already measured
them on four seeds; (c) is the open one, and I expect it to fail for a
majority of seeds, because an eccentricity factor of 30.6 multiplies the
step count at periapsis where the engine is already slowest.
